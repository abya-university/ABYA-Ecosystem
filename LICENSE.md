## Part IV: Course Content and Creator Intellectual Property

### 4.1 Creator Ownership

**Creators own 100% of the intellectual property in the courses they publish on ABYA Nexus.** ABYA Nexus Foundation does not claim any ownership over creator-produced educational content, regardless of how it is hosted, delivered, or monetized through the platform.

When a creator publishes a course on ABYA Nexus, they grant ABYA Nexus Foundation a **non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license** to:
- Host and store the course content on ABYA servers or IPFS
- Deliver the course content to enrolled learners
- Promote the course through ABYA marketing channels
- Allow enrolled learners to access the content for personal, non-commercial use

This license is:
- **Non-exclusive** — Creators may publish the same content on Coursera, Udemy, YouTube, or any other platform simultaneously
- **Revocable** — Creators may remove their content from ABYA with 30 days' written notice
- **Non-transferable** — ABYA may not sublicense creator content to third parties without explicit written permission

### 4.2 IP-NFT Mechanics and Royalties

When a creator mints their curriculum as an IP-NFT on the AcademyFacet:

- The creator receives **65% of all primary enrollment revenue** in perpetuity, distributed automatically by the smart contract
- The creator receives **2% of all secondary market royalties** when their IP-NFT is traded on secondary markets, in perpetuity
- The IP-NFT represents the creator's ownership record on-chain — it is a digital title deed, not a transfer of copyright

Minting a curriculum as an IP-NFT does not transfer copyright to ABYA, to the NFT buyer, or to any other party. Copyright remains with the creator at all times.

### 4.3 Learner Content and ZK Credentials

Assessment submissions, quiz responses, project work, and other content created by learners during the learning process:
- Is **owned by the learner** who created it
- Is **never stored in plaintext on-chain** — only cryptographic commitments and ZK proofs are stored
- May be retained by ABYA in encrypted form for credential verification purposes with the learner's consent
- May be permanently deleted by the learner upon request (subject to the ABYA Privacy Policy)
